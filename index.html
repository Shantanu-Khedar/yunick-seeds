About Us

Yunick Seeds, by Yunick Agro, Hisar

At Yunick Seeds, we believe a good harvest starts with a good seed. Based in Dabra, Hisar (Haryana), Yunick Agro produces and supplies high-yield, quality-tested seeds of wheat, paddy, oats, jowar, moong and mustard. Farmers across Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh trust our seeds.

Every batch is cleaned, graded, tested and treated at our own registered processing plant before it reaches the farmer. Our goal is simple: अधिक पैदावार, उत्तम गुणवत्ता वाले बीज — higher yield, better quality seeds.

Advice from the field

Wheat · Rabi

Which wheat variety for which sowing date?

Timely sowing (late Oct to mid Nov): HD-2967, WH-1270, DBW-187, DBW-303, DBW-327, DBW-222, PBW-826. Late sowing after paddy or cotton (late Nov to Dec): HD-3386, WH-1309, DBW-872. Rainfed on conserved moisture: C-306. Use 40 kg seed per acre for timely and 50 kg for late sowing.

Paddy · Kharif

Raising a healthy basmati nursery

Sow the nursery in the last week of May to mid June for PB-1121 and PB-1718, and up to end June for the shorter PB-1509. Use 4 to 5 kg seed per acre of main field, keep the nursery bed moist not flooded, and transplant 25 to 30 day old seedlings at 2 per hill.

Fodder · all year

Green fodder for every season

Sow Super-17 multicut jowar from March to July and take the first cut at 55 to 60 days, then every 30 to 35 days. For winter, sow oats in October–November for cuttings through to March.
